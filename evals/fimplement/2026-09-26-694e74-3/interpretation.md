# Interpretation — fimplement, opus-5.5[1m] vs opus-5[1m], medium, no serena, 4 replicas

- Machine overload confounds this run: 8 arms in parallel pinned the CPU (0% idle, ~8 concurrent rustc), so long `make audit`/clippy calls outran the Bash tool timeout. 4 of 8 arms moved the audit to the background, ended the headless turn "waiting" and never committed (5.5: r1, r2, r3; 5: r2). Wall/active time and output tokens are not model-representative — the stalled arms ended early, which is why Opus 5 shows "significantly" longer time (+340 s) and more tokens (+8.7k).
- Under the same load, Opus 5 finished 3 of 4 (commits, green audit, honest report) vs Opus 5.5 1 of 4. The stall itself is a conduct choice (background + end turn instead of waiting in the foreground), so this leans toward Opus 5 being more robust headless — but n=4 under uneven load; not significant (check_pass Δ 0.25, rank Δ −2, major defects Δ −0.75, all n.s.).
- Code quality does not separate the models: all 8 runs match the reference implementation; compare-judge defects on stalled arms are "nothing committed", plus 5.5-r2's real rustfmt miss (`cmd/agentwt/tests/scenarios/mod.rs:499`) and two MINOR test-fixture weakenings (5-r3 `--force`, 5.5-r3 dropped lock). Top 4 ranks: 5-r4, 5-r1, 5.5-r4, 5-r3 — exactly the committed arms.
- Harness artifacts, not arm defects: post-run checks of 5.5-r1 and 5-r2 failed on a parallel golangci-lint lock collision, not on code. Smoke fails everywhere on permission denials (reads into sibling arm worktrees and compound greps) that did not block work. Cost ($0.48–$8.47) varies more within arms than between them.
- Step adherence: the consistent misses across both models are unchanged from the earlier high-effort run — `/verify` never invoked (not in this environment's skill listing) and the risk loci S7/S8 never executed; per-unit gating was batched per language in every arm. Only one `violated` verdict (5-r1: declared "Done" with V3–V8 open) and one in 5.5-r2 (gate before advance).

Worker pins: no arm spawned a subagent; `/package-commit` ran inline on the arm model. Judge caveat: the adherence judge for 5-r3 (label H) read `run.json`, which maps labels to models — that judge was not fully blind. For a clean rerun cap concurrency at 4 arms.
